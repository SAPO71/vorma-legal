VORMA — Privacy Policy

Controller

Service provider / data controller: Salih Postaçı Address: İstanbul, Türkiye Contact: salihpostaci@icloud.com

Data and purpose

Identity, name, email and optional profile image come from your sign-in provider. Workout and nutrition logs are stored for your account on the device and synchronized with Firebase when cloud sync is enabled. Your own program, measurements and food library are stored on the device. Profile targets, support messages and account actions may be sent to relevant service providers.

Optional health data

Apple Health / Health Connect requires a separate in-app choice and operating-system permission. Authorized steps, active energy, weight, resting heart rate and sleep are summarized on-device. Completed workouts can be written to the health platform. Health data is not used for advertising.

Providers and transfers

Google/Firebase provides authentication and cloud storage; Apple/Google provides sign-in and store payments. VORMA does not receive card details. These providers may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States. Such transfers rely on the providers’ data processing terms and standard contractual clauses, in line with applicable law.

Advertising and retention

The free version shows Google AdMob ads. Only non-personalized ads are requested and VORMA does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Any required regional consent prompt is shown before ads are requested. AdMob may process device and app information to serve ads, prevent fraud and measure performance. Premium or Remove Ads turns ads off. Data is retained as needed for the relevant functions. Account deletion attempts to remove workout, nutrition and personal-record data and close the account, reporting failures for retry. Store transaction records are handled by the store. Records required by law may be retained only for that purpose.

Controls and security

Account-based access controls and encrypted network connections are used. Local logs remain in device storage; protect your device. You may turn off optional health access and notifications, correct your profile or request deletion and other privacy rights. Contact: salihpostaci@icloud.com
