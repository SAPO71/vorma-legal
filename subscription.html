VORMA — Subscription and Purchase Terms

Provider and store

Service provider / data controller: Salih Postaçı Address: İstanbul, Türkiye Contact: salihpostaci@icloud.com Optional premium purchases require separate Apple App Store or Google Play confirmation. VORMA does not receive card details.

Price and renewal

Monthly and annual plans may be offered. Binding price, currency, taxes and billing period are displayed by the store. Estimates shown while sales are unavailable are not a payment offer. Annual total is displayed prominently. Subscriptions renew for the same period unless cancelled in the store before its deadline.

14-day trial

The trial is 14 days for eligible accounts on a premium store offer. Eligibility and prior use are determined by the store. If no eligible 14-day offer is available, no free trial is promised. Unless cancelled in time, the price for the selected billing period is charged after the trial.

Cancellation, restore and refunds

Manage cancellation in Apple Account > Subscriptions or Google Play > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions. Deleting the app, signing out or deleting a VORMA account does not cancel store billing. Restore checks valid purchases for the same store account. Request refunds from the charging store; mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.
